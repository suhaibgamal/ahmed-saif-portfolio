# Ahmed Saif Portfolio

Official bilingual portfolio website for Ahmed Saif (`a7mdsif.com`), Yemeni singer, composer, and music producer.

## 10 Bespoke Design Archetypes

1. **Aureate Lyricist** (`style-1` / `aureate`): Royal Yemeni Maestro & Classical Gold filigree.
2. **Nocturnal Resonance** (`style-2` / `nocturne`): Cyber-Acoustic Resonator, Cyan & Neon telemetry.
3. **Analog Tape Lounge** (`style-3` / `tape`): Raw 70s Acoustic Reel-to-Reel Session & Tube Amber.
4. **Atelier Forest** (`style-4` / `atelier`): Swiss Architectural Grid & Forest Slate.
5. **Monochrome Gallery** (`style-5` / `gallery`): Brutalist Museum Exhibition Archival & Stark Typography.
6. **Neo-Bento Showcase** (`style-6` / `bento`): Modern Modular Product Dashboard & Dynamic Audio Widgets.
7. **Editorial Lookbook** (`style-7` / `editorial`): Haute Horlogerie & Fashion Spread.
8. **Analog Studio Console** (`style-8` / `studio`): Hardware DAW Console, VU Needle Meters & Rack Units.
9. **Live Stage Headliner** (`style-9` / `stage`): Stadium Concert Tour & Festival Headliner Aura.
10. **Minimal Audio Canvas** (`style-10` / `canvas`): Zen Typographic Purity & Soot Ink Stillness.
